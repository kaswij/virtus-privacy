Virtus Privacy Policy Last updated: 26 September 2026 Virtus ("the app", "we", "us") is a fitness app for logging workouts and food, and for sharing your progress with people you choose. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have. It is written to be read, so we have kept it plain. 1. Information we collect Information you give us: Account details: your email address and password (passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider and are never visible to us). Profile details: a username, profile picture, and, if you choose to add them, your full name, age, sex, height and weight. Your full name, age, sex, height and weight are private and are never shown to other users. Fitness and food data: workouts, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, cardio activities, food you log, custom foods, and your calorie and macronutrient goals. Photos and videos you attach to workouts and diet posts. Social content: posts you share, captions, comments, likes, who you follow, who follows you, and people you block. Reports you send us about content or behaviour you find inappropriate. Coach applications: if you apply to become a coach, the qualification documents and details you submit. Information from your device: the app asks permission before using your camera (to scan food barcodes) and your photo library (to choose photos and videos). We only use these when you take those actions. We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifier, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers. 2. How we use your information To run the app: to sign you in, save and show your workouts and food logs, and calculate your progress and recommended daily nutrient intakes (which use your age and sex). To let you share and interact with other people, as described in section 3. To keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and removing content or accounts that break the rules. To review coach applications. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. 3. Who can see your information Private information: your email, full name, age, sex, height, weight, food log, workout history and goals are visible only to you. They are only shown to others if you choose to share a specific workout or diet summary. Posts you share: a workout or diet summary you post, including its photos, videos, caption, comments and likes, can be seen by people who follow you and whose follow you have accepted (or by anyone who follows you, if your account is public), and by your coach if you have an active coaching relationship. A diet summary post includes the foods and nutrition totals saved at the time you posted it. Public profile: your username, profile picture and whether you are a coach can be found by other signed-in users who search for you. Private accounts: if you switch your account to private, people must send a follow request that you approve. Blocking: you can block anyone. Blocking removes any follows between you. 4. Services we use We use a small number of trusted services to run the app. They process information only to provide their service to us: Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system and file storage, where the information described above is kept. Open Food Facts (a free, open food database) receives the barcode or search words you enter when you look up a packaged food. This is not linked to your account. Expo is used to build and deliver the app and its updates. Apple (TestFlight and the App Store) distributes the app. The app also includes data from the Australian Food Composition Database (Food Standards Australia New Zealand), which is bundled in the app and does not receive any of your information. 5. Keeping your information and deleting it We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your login, your profile, your workouts, food logs, posts, comments, likes, follows and your uploaded photos, videos and documents. Deleted information may remain in secure backups for a limited time before it is removed. Reports you sent about others may be kept for safety and legal reasons. You can also edit your profile, remove individual posts ("Unshare"), and delete your own comments in the app at any time. 6. Your choices and rights You can access and change most of your information in the app. You may also ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us at the email address below. If you live somewhere with privacy laws that give you additional rights, such as the Australian Privacy Principles, we will respect them. 7. Security Your information is stored with access rules so that other users can only see what is described in section 3. Photos and videos you attach to workouts and diet posts, and coach documents, are stored privately and are only available to people allowed to see the post they belong to. The exception is your profile picture: it is stored so that anyone who has its web address can view it, so choose a picture you are comfortable with others seeing. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it to yourself. 8. Children The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. 9. Health information Virtus is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Nutrient targets are general guides based on published recommended intakes for your age and sex. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making significant changes to your diet or exercise. 10. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. When we make significant changes we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, tell you in the app. 11.
